Wheel and tire brush.

This is a soft-bristle brush for washing motorcycle wheels and tires. The bristles reach around the barrel of the rim. They are not a wire brush, and they are not for brake discs.

Philippine roads put a brown film on the wheel in a week: brake dust, road grime, and whatever the tire flings up in the rain. A sponge wipes the face of the rim and misses the inside. A stiff kitchen brush scratches clear coat and leaves swirl marks on a cast wheel. The soft blue bristles are the middle path.

What the set is for

  • The face of the rim and the barrel behind the spokes.
  • The tire sidewall, where brown dust hides the lettering.
  • More than one brush size in the 3-in-1 set, so a scooter wheel and a wider tire do not share one head.

Keep it off the brake disc and the caliper piston. Bristles and soap do not belong on the rotor. Wash those with a clean rag if you must, then ride gently until the disc is dry.

A simple wash

  • Rinse the wheel first so the grit is not ground into the paint.
  • Work the brush with motorcycle soap or a mild car soap. Skip household bleach and oven cleaner.
  • Rinse until the water runs clear, including inside the barrel.
  • Dry the brake disc if you soaked it, and do not park the bike on a fresh disc with the brake clamped overnight.

Check this before you open the product

  • You want soft bristles, not a metal wheel brush.
  • The handle is long enough to reach a rear wheel without your knuckles on the chain.
  • You already have soap. The brush does not replace it.
