Helmets · 3 October 2026

EVO GT-PRO full-face.

The shop title is EVO GT-PRO plain full-face, dual visor, with a free clear lens. It is not the TR-X half-face. Buy it from that Shopee listing.

The listing says the visor pair is clear and tinted, the shell is ABS composite, and the foam is a washable Dura-flex set. It also prints DOT certified and ICC certified. Treat those as the seller’s words. A full-face shell still needs the size that matches your head, and the approval sticker still has to be on the helmet you unpack.

How the shop says to size it

  • Measure about one centimetre above the eyebrows and ears.
  • Ask someone else to read the tape.
  • Adjust if your hair is long or thick, or if your cheeks are large.

The listing says returns are discouraged unless the mistake is on the shop. Read that policy on the page before you pay.

Check this before you buy

  • You want a full-face GT-PRO, not a half-face TR-X.
  • The size variant matches the measurement, not a guess from a hat size.
  • You will check the ICC sticker on the shell for road use in the Philippines.

Kurbada does not certify this helmet.
