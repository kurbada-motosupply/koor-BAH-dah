SOLOEVER mesh balaclava.

A stretch hood for sun, dust, and the gap under a helmet. It fits the rider, not one motorcycle. The photos in this guide are the seller’s pictures of the black version.

What the photos show

  • A full head and neck, with a mesh panel over the mouth.
  • An opening shaped for glasses or goggles.
  • The listing title says UPF50+. That claim belongs to the seller. Kurbada has not tested the fabric.
  • The first photo prints a buy-1-take-1 offer. Promotions change. Read the price block on the listing before you pay.

How to wear it

Pull it on before the helmet, then check that the mesh sits over the mouth and that the helmet strap still reaches the jaw. If you wear glasses, the arm of the frame needs the opening shown in the detail photo. It is not a helmet and it does not replace an ICC sticker on the shell.
