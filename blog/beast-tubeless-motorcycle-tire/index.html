Beast tubeless tire, with sealant and a valve.

Beast Flash and Beast Warrior are tubeless motorcycle tires listed in 13 inch, 14 inch, and 17 inch. The catalog set includes tire sealant and a valve, often called a pito.

The inch number is only the rim diameter. The full size is the line on your current sidewall, something like 80/90-14 or 110/70-17. Order the size that is already on the bike unless a fitter has told you a different width will clear the swingarm and the fender.

Which diameter is which bike

  • 13 inch and 14 inch cover a lot of scooters and small underbones. Check the sidewall. Do not guess from the model name.
  • 17 inch is the usual front or rear on many naked bikes and small sport bikes. Front and rear sizes are often different. Read both wheels.

A tubeless tire needs a tubeless rim. If your wheel still runs an inner tube, a tubeless casing is the wrong buy until the rim is tubeless too.

What comes with it

  • The Beast tire in the diameter you select.
  • Sealant. That is for small punctures in a tubeless tire. It is not a patch for a cut sidewall.
  • A valve. Replace the old rubber valve if it is cracked. A new tire on a tired valve just leaks at the stem.

Check this before you open the product

  • Write down the full size from the sidewall, not only 13, 14, or 17.
  • Confirm the wheel is tubeless.
  • Look at the tread. Cords showing, or a flat spot the width of the contact patch, means the tire is finished. Waiting on a slow leak is how a scooter sits in traffic on the canvas.
  • Plan the mount. A tire this size is seated with a proper machine or by someone who does it every day. Do not pry it on with a screwdriver against the rim edge.

Match the full size and a compound you can mount. The name on the sidewall does not change the size printed there.
