AYOTO BeAT headlamp.

The listing is an AYOTO bi-LED headlamp for the Honda BeAT. The title names BeAT FI V1, V2, and V3. The checkout note says to choose BeAT FI or BeAT ESP. Those are different variants.

The seller photo shows two BEAT signal lenses, one blue and one amber, plus night shots of a BeAT front. The written listing covers both the headlamp and the turn signals. It names a 1.5 inch bi-LED that switches high and low, demon eyes, a running DRL, and a socket-to-socket plug. It also says the stock lamp can stay in storage.

What it fits

  • Honda BeAT FI, the variant the listing asks you to select.
  • Honda BeAT ESP, listed as its own variant with its own color lines.

A Click lamp and an NMAX lamp are other products. ESP and standard BeAT panels do not share every part, which is why the seller asks you to check the bike before checkout.

Check this before you open the product

  • The papers or the body say BeAT FI or BeAT ESP. Pick that variant.
  • You read the color note. The seller says demon-eye and DRL colors are sent from available stock unless you message them first and ask for a specific pair.
  • The color lists are split. BeAT ESP and BeAT FI do not print the same DRL and demon-eye pairs.
  • You keep the original lamp until the new one plugs in and aims where you ride.

Road use

An aftermarket headlamp is not automatically approved for a public road. Aim and glare still matter at night. Kurbada does not certify this lamp. Brightness lines on the listing are the seller’s claims.
