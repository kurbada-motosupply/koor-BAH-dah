4-in-1 jump starter.

The shop title is a 4-in-1 jump starter, 20000mAh, with an air pump and a power bank, for a car battery. Buy it from that Shopee listing. It is not a motorcycle-only part.

The listing prints two battery lines: 10000mAh, and a 3.0 max of 20000mAh. It says both boxes share the same outside size, 181 by 93 by 75 millimetres, and a weight of 890 grams. It says the 10000mAh unit is for 12V cars at 3.0 litres and below, and the 20000mAh unit is for 12V cars at 3.0 litres and above. Starting current is printed as 600A and peak current as 3000A. Those figures are the seller’s specification. Kurbada has not tested them.

What the shop says is in the unit

  • A jump-start function for a 12V car battery.
  • An air pump. The listing prints about 40 litres a minute, 3 to 150 psi.
  • A USB power bank output printed as 5V at 2A.

A scooter battery is not the 3.0 litre car engine this page describes. If you planned to jump a motorcycle, read the shop’s applicable-model line and ask them before you treat a bike battery like a car battery. Clamp polarity still matters. A reversed clamp can damage electronics.

Check this before you buy

  • You picked 10000mAh or 20000mAh on purpose. The title and the spec list both appear.
  • The vehicle is a 12V car in the range the variant names.
  • You have a way to charge the pack before you need it. A stored pack runs down.
