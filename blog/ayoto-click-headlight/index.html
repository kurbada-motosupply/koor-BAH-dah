AYOTO Click headlamp.

The listing title names an AYOTO bi-LED headlamp for Honda Click V2 and V3, and for Vario 125 and 150. The package line says it is a full headlamp set for the new Vario and Click 125 and 150.

The seller photo shows the lamp lit, with a phone on a color wheel underneath. The written specs name a 90 watt set, four bi-LED matrix lights, a welcome light when the key turns, a flat cutoff, leveling, a white hexa shroud, and red or blue demon eyes chosen by variant. The same text says the original lamp can be stored, and that the harness includes a fuse. The title also says Bluetooth app control. Confirm that on the variant you select, because the spec list describes a switch for DRL, main light, and high beam.

What it fits

  • Honda Click V2 and Click V3, as named in the title.
  • The Vario 125 and Vario 150 names printed on the same listing. That is the seller’s fitment line for this lamp.

Click 125 and Click 160 are different bikes. Click 160 is not in this title. A BeAT lamp and an NMAX lamp are other products. ADV150 and ADV160 are not named here either.

Check this before you open the product

  • The side cover says Click V2, Click V3, or the 125 or 150 the listing calls Vario.
  • You pick the demon-eye color on the variant. The listing says red or blue, and that the DRL indicator follows that color.
  • You read the phone picture as the seller’s photo. If you need app control, confirm it in the variant text before you pay.
  • You keep the stock lamp until the new one plugs in and the beam sits where you want it. The listing says the set includes leveling.

Road use

An aftermarket headlamp is not automatically approved for a public road. Aim the beam so it does not wash out oncoming riders. Kurbada does not certify this lamp. Watt and brightness lines stay on the seller’s page.
