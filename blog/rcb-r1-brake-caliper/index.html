RCB R1 brake caliper.

The shop title is RCB R1 series brake caliper, four piston, universal, right hand. That Shopee listing is the shop to buy it from. A caliper still needs the bracket that matches your fork.

The listing says this R1 caliper can use a Brembo P4 caliper bracket. It describes a two-piece forged body, four fastening screws, and dual-size pistons: 34 millimetres times two on the leading side and 30 millimetres times two on the trailing side. It says the part is right hand. Those measurements are the seller’s. Kurbada has not fitted one.

What to match

  • The side. This listing says right hand. A left caliper is a different part.
  • The bracket. Universal does not mean every fork. The shop points at a Brembo P4 bracket.
  • The hose, the pads, and the master cylinder. A four-piston body can need a different line and a different fluid level.

A brake part that does not sit square will drag or pull. If the mount holes do not meet the bracket, stop. Do not slot the fork to make it fit.

Check this before you buy

  • You need the right-side caliper, not a pair and not a left.
  • You already know which bracket the fork uses, or you will ask the shop before checkout.
  • You can bleed the brake after the swap. The listing does not include a bleed procedure.

Kurbada does not certify this caliper for a public road. The button opens the shop.
