Helmets · 3 October 2026

EVO TR-X Radix half-face.

This is the EVO TR-X Radix half-face, a different listing from the plain TR-X. The shop says dual visor, a free clear lens, DOT, and ICC. Buy the Radix from this Shopee page, not from the plain one.

The feature list matches the plain TR-X on paper: ABS composite shell, dual visor, aerodynamic vents, and a washable Dura-flex foam. The graphic is the Radix. DOT and ICC are printed by the seller. Check the sticker on the helmet you receive.

How the shop says to size it

  • Wrap a tape about one centimetre above the ears and one centimetre above the eyebrows.
  • Let someone else read the number, at the forehead.
  • Allow extra room for long or thick hair and for large cheeks.

A half-face lid does not cover the chin. If you wanted the GT-PRO, that is the full-face listing.

Check this before you buy

  • The variant photo is the Radix graphic you want.
  • The size matches a fresh measurement.
  • You read the shop’s return line before you pay.

Kurbada does not certify this helmet.
