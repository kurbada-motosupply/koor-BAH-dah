Nomad Camino spare visor, V-27.

The Flash-series V-27 visor is a spare shield for the Nomad Camino helmet. It is not a universal visor for every full-face lid in the Philippines.

Riders look for a Nomad Camino spare visor when the original shield is scratched, cracked, or too dark for night riding. The V-27 shape follows that shell: two side pivots and a center tab that locks into the Camino brow. If those three points do not line up with the helmet you already own, this visor will not seal.

What it fits

  • Nomad Camino helmets that take the V-27 visor.
  • Flash-series tint. The shield in the catalog photo shifts from gold to blue depending on the light.

It does not fit a generic HJC, LS2, KYT, or NHK shell just because those are also full-face helmets. Visor mounts are model-specific.

Check this before you open the product

  • The helmet name on the shell or the box says Nomad Camino.
  • The old visor has the same side-hole pattern as the V-27.
  • The helmet still has a valid ICC sticker if you use it on Philippine roads. A new visor does not replace a missing sticker.
  • You want a spare, not a different helmet size. The visor does not change shell size.

How a visor swap usually goes

Open both side pivots, lift the old shield off, and seat the new one until the center tab clicks. Do this in the shade so you can see whether the seal meets the eye port all the way around. If one side sits proud of the rubber, the mount is wrong and the visor should come back off.

A mirrored Flash tint is bright at noon and darker in the rain. Keep a clear shield in the seat if you ride after sunset.
