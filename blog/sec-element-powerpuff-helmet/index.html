Helmets · 3 October 2026

SEC Element modular.

The shop title is the SEC Motosupply Element modular helmet in the Powerpuff Girls graphic. It is its own listing, separate from the Revive V1 and the Whirlwind. Buy that graphic from this Shopee shop.

The listing calls it a modular dual-visor helmet for commuting, with a flip-up design, interior padding, and a locking mechanism. The graphic is the Powerpuff Girls edition the seller printed. The captured listing text does not name an ICC sticker. Check the shell you receive before road use. Kurbada does not certify the helmet.

What to match

  • The character graphic on the variant. Blossom, Bubbles, and Buttercup are named in the shop copy as the theme, not as three helmet structures.
  • Head size. A graphic does not change the fit.
  • The chin-bar lock. Ride with it closed.

Check this before you buy

  • This is the Element graphic you want, not a plain modular from another SEC link.
  • The size on the variant matches a measurement taken above the brows.
  • You will look for the approval mark on the helmet, not only in the title.
