Helmets · 3 October 2026

SEC Revive V1 modular.

The shop title is SEC Motosupply Revive V1 modular helmet, a flip-up shell with a wide visor. That Shopee listing is the shop to buy it from. It is a different lid from the Whirlwind, the Element, and the Zephyr.

The listing says the chin bar flips up, the shell is a durable outer, and the package is one Revive V1 plus one clear visor. It also mentions ventilation, interior padding, and a retention system. It does not, in the text captured from the page, name an ICC sticker. For road use in the Philippines, look for a valid ICC mark on the helmet you receive. Kurbada has not certified this shell.

What a flip-up changes

  • The chin bar has to lock closed while you ride. An unlocked bar is not a full-face.
  • Glasses are easier to manage, which is why riders compare modular lids.
  • The size still comes from a head measurement, not from the photo.

Check this before you buy

  • You want the Revive V1, not another SEC modular on a different listing.
  • The size options on the shop match your measurement.
  • You will inspect the lock and the approval sticker when the box arrives.
