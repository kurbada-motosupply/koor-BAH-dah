REV exhaust pipe.

The shop title is REV exhaust power pipe for Click 125 to 150G, Click 160, M3, Sporty, Soulty, and BeAT FI, plus Aerox V2. The written list is longer. Buy the variant that names your motorcycle from that Shopee shop.

The listing says stainless elbow, adjustable sound, and plug and play. It also says the shop may send a photo of the actual unit before shipping, and that color in photos can shift with the light. A no-cancellation, no-return line is on the page. Read it.

Models named on the listing

  • Honda Click 125, Click 150G, and Click 160.
  • Honda BeAT FI, ADV160, and PCX160.
  • Yamaha Aerox V2, NMAX V2 2020, and the M3, Sporty, and Soulty names.
  • Suzuki Burgman.

BeAT ESP is not the same phrase as BeAT FI. Aerox V1 is not Aerox V2. NMAX V1 is not the NMAX V2 2020 line. ADV150 and PCX150 are not the 160s in this list. A pipe for the wrong header will not bolt up even when the canister looks the same. This is a different pipe from the JVT listing already in the catalog.

Check this before you buy

  • The variant title includes your model and version, not only the word REV.
  • You know whether the bike is FI, and whether it is V1 or V2.
  • You accept that an aftermarket pipe is not automatically approved for a public road. Sound rules still apply.

Kurbada does not tune the pipe and does not certify it. The button opens the shop.
