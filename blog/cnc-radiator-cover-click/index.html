CNC radiator cover.

The shop lists a CNC radiator cover for Honda Click 125i and 150, Vario 125 and 150, plus ADV150 and PCX150. That Shopee listing is where you buy it.

The listing says the cover is aluminum alloy and prints a size of 196 by 270 by 19 millimetres. The package line says one cover and three bolts. It also says the piece is a guard for the radiator and a visual cover. Kurbada has not measured the panel.

What the title names

  • Honda Click 125i and Click 150.
  • Honda Vario 125 and Vario 150.
  • Honda ADV150 and PCX150.

ADV160, PCX160, and Click 160 are not in that list. A later body can use a different radiator opening. If the badge on your side panel is 160, do not assume this cover bolts on.

Check this before you buy

  • The model on the bike is one of the names in the title, including the 150, not a 160.
  • You want a radiator guard, not a crankcase cover and not a headlamp.
  • You can reach the stock bolts the shop says the three included bolts replace.

Kurbada does not stock the cover. The button opens the shop.
