Helmets · 3 October 2026

EVO TR-X plain half-face.

This is the plain EVO TR-X half-face, sold by Evo Helmet Philippines. It is a different lid from the TR-X Radix and from the GT-PRO full-face. The button opens that shop.

The listing says the shell is ABS composite, with a dual visor, aerodynamic vents, and a Dura-flex washable foam. It also says a clear lens is included. DOT and ICC appear in the feature list. Those marks are the seller’s claim. Look at the sticker on the helmet you receive before you use it on a public road.

How the shop says to size it

  • Measure about one centimetre above the eyebrows and one centimetre above the ears.
  • Have someone else read the number.
  • Move up a size for long or thick hair, or for large cheeks.

The same shop prints a no-return rule unless the mistake is theirs. Read that line before you pay. A half-face shell leaves the chin open. It is not the GT-PRO full-face.

Check this before you buy

  • You want the plain TR-X, not the Radix graphic.
  • You measured your head, and the size on the variant matches that number.
  • You can see an ICC sticker requirement for the road you ride, and you will check the shell you receive.

Kurbada does not certify this helmet. Price and stock stay on the Shopee shop.
