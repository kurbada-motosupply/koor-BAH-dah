Smash 110 and 115 handle bolts.

This is a six-piece HENG / SC CNC Siam front handle bolt set for the Suzuki Smash 110 and Smash 115. The bolts replace the screws you can see on the handle cover, not the engine bolts underneath.

Smash riders change these screws when the stock heads rust, strip, or just look plain next to a new cover. The set in the catalog is the flower, or gear, head style: six CNC bolts sold as one set. HENG and SC Siam are the names on the hardware. They are made as dress-up and replacement screws, so the thread still has to match the hole you are filling.

What it fits

  • Suzuki Smash 110.
  • Suzuki Smash 115.
  • The front handle cover screws, six places, not the caliper or the engine case.

A Smash bolt is not a Honda Click bolt and not a Wave bolt. Underbones share a look. They do not share screw lengths.

Check this before you open the product

  • The side cover or the OR/CR says Smash 110 or Smash 115.
  • You can count six handle-cover screws, the same places circled on the product photo.
  • You keep the old bolts until the new ones thread in by hand. If a bolt tightens in two turns and stops, the length is wrong.
  • You tighten them snug, not until the plastic cover creaks. CNC heads look strong. The cover under them is not.

Why riders pick this set

The stock screws on a Smash that lives outside go dull fast. A matched set keeps the six heads the same color. Replace all six together. Mixing two new bolts with four rusty ones is how the cover starts to look unfinished.
