Luggage · 3 October 2026

SEC Diplomat and Freedom box.

One Shopee shop listing covers the SEC alloy Diplomat and the hard plastic SEC Freedom 45 litre. That listing is where you buy it. Choose the box on the variant, then match the bracket to the motorcycle.

The listing says the inclusions are a backrest, a baseplate, leather linings, keys, and bolts and nuts. It also says the bracket is separate and depends on the type of motorcycle, and that a bracket may be available from the same shop. A universal baseplate is not a rack. The rack has to meet the grab rail or the subframe on your bike.

What to pick

  • Alloy Diplomat or hard plastic Freedom 45L. They are named together, and they are not the same shell.
  • The bracket for your model. Message the shop with the motorcycle name before you assume the bolts in the box are enough.
  • This is not the ABS hard-plastic listing that offers 28, 38, and 45 litres.

A top box changes how the bike feels in a crosswind. Load it low, and do not drill a grab rail that was not made for a rack.

Check this before you buy

  • You chose Diplomat or Freedom on the variant, not only the photo.
  • You know which bracket the shop says fits your motorcycle.
  • The 45 litre figure is the Freedom size in the title. Confirm capacity on the variant you select.
