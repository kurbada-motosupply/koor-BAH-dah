Origine Vega Distinguished full-face helmet.

A retro full-face lid with a fiberglass shell. The catalog photo shows matte black and glossy cream. Confirm the finish and the shell size on the product page before you open it.

Riders look for the Origine Vega when they want a round, classic full face instead of a sharp sport shell. It is a complete helmet, not a spare visor. The Nomad Camino V-27 visor does not mount on this shell.

What the product lists

  • Fiberglass shell.
  • Double D-ring closure.
  • Removable, washable interior.
  • Adjustable vents.
  • A visor with anti-scratch and UV coating, plus a lock that keeps the shield down while you ride.
  • Listed weight of about 1,450 g, give or take 50 g.
  • European road approval cited on the product page as E2206, the ECE 22.06 standard in use from 2023.

Color and size

The photo in the catalog shows two finishes side by side: matte black and glossy cream. Those are two color options, not a pair that ships together. Pick one finish on the product page.

Measure around the fullest part of the head, just above the eyebrows and ears, and match that number to the size chart on the product page. A retro shell often sits closer than a modern sport lid. If you wear glasses, check whether the cheek pads leave a channel for the arms. The listing does not publish a glasses-fit note.

Riding it in the Philippines

Enforcement on Philippine roads looks for a valid ICC sticker on the helmet you are wearing. The product page cites the European ECE 22.06 approval. That mark and an ICC sticker are not the same thing. Before you use the helmet on a public road, confirm the sticker on the shell you receive.

A full-face shell is the better default for highway speed. The visor lock is there so the shield stays seated. Seat the strap with the double D-ring under the jaw, then tug the loose end. If the helmet lifts off when you tip your head forward with the strap done up, the size is too large.
