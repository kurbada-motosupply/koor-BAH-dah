SEC ABS top box.

The shop title lists an SEC Motosupply ABS hard plastic top box in 28, 38, and 45 litres. The written copy on the page describes a 45 litre box. Buy the litre size from that Shopee variant.

The description says black and silver, two keys, bolts, nuts, washers, a backrest, and a baseplate. It also says plug and play. A baseplate still needs a rack that belongs to your motorcycle. This listing is not the alloy Diplomat and Freedom 45 litre listing.

What to confirm

  • 28, 38, or 45 litres. The title names all three. The paragraph you read may only describe the 45.
  • The bracket for your grab rail. Easy to install still depends on the model.
  • Color. The copy says black and silver. Match the photo on the variant.

Do not drill a new hole in the bodywork to force a baseplate. If the shop sells the bracket separately, ask which one fits the bike you ride.

Check this before you buy

  • The variant litre matches the space you actually need.
  • You have the motorcycle name ready if the bracket is not in the box.
  • You are not mixing this up with the Diplomat alloy box.
